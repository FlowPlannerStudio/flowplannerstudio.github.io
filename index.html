Frequently asked questions

Questions before getting started

Does Flow Video Planner post without my permission?

No. You authorize the account and choose the video, caption, privacy setting, and publishing workflow.

Do I give the service my account password?

No. Account connection uses the platform's official authorization screen.

Can the service guarantee views or prevent moderation?

No. Moderation, eligibility, distribution, and view counts remain controlled by the connected platform.

How can I get help?

Open the application to review status and guidance, or use the project's support page.
